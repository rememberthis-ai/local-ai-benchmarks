# Text to speech: three voice memos

Three short memos from open-synthetic-identities (one English, two German), each read by every model in an invented voice. Where a model can design a voice from a description it got the description; otherwise the closest preset. Every take was transcribed with whisper large-v3-turbo and compared with its script: WER is the share of script words missing, swapped or added, and a few percent is whisper's own noise. 26 October 2026; local models on the M1 Max through mlx-audio 0.6.1.
